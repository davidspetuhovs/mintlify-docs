OpenBat is a conversational analytics platform for AI chatbots. It captures conversations between your end-users and your chatbot, runs automated analysis on them, and surfaces the results as dashboards, reports, search, and alerts. The goal is to answer product, customer-success, and support questions — like which customers are at risk of churning or what users are asking for — rather than engineering questions about token counts.

## The capture, analyze, surface loop

Data moves through OpenBat in three stages:

- **Capture** — Your SDK integration sends messages to the ingestion endpoint (`POST /api/v1/capture`) using a chatbot-specific API key (format `ob_live_*`). Each captured message is stored against its conversation.
- **Analyze** — A configurable AI pipeline runs analysis on captured messages. You define what gets analyzed — sentiment, intent, flags, outcomes, AI literacy, and personas — and the results are stored per message, with multiple analysis types allowed on a single message.
- **Surface** — Analysis results appear in the conversation detail view, aggregate into the overview dashboard and reports, power Deep Search and filtering, and trigger workflows when conditions are met.

The analysis pipeline is configurable rather than fixed: you add custom flags, outcome types, and calibration examples in **Analysis Config**, so the pipeline detects patterns specific to your product. Changes apply to future conversations, and you can re-analyze existing ones.

## Core entities

OpenBat organizes data in a hierarchy that scopes everything to your organization. The relationships below are inferred from the URL structure and data model and likely reflect the underlying schema:

- **Organization** — The top-level tenant. Your team members belong to it, and all data is scoped to it. You can switch organizations from the sidebar org switcher.
- **Chatbot** — An independent analytics unit inside an organization. Each chatbot has its own API key, conversation history, analysis configuration, workflows, reports, and settings. An organization can have many chatbots.
- **Conversation** — A thread of messages between one end-user and the chatbot. Conversations belong to a chatbot.
- **Message** — A single user or assistant turn within a conversation.
- **Analysis** — The results produced by the pipeline for a message. A message can carry several analyses of different types.

Two more entity directories are derived from captured data: **Users** (the external end-users who talk to the chatbot, with health and AI literacy scores) and **Organizations** (external customer orgs, populated automatically when your SDK sends organization metadata).

## The kinds of analysis

The pipeline produces several kinds of analysis, configured in **Analysis Config** under the User Analysis and Assistant Analysis tabs:

- **Sentiment** — Scored sentiment on user messages.
- **Intent and query types** — User messages are clustered into canonical question categories you can browse on the Query Types page.
- **Flags** — Tagged signals on conversations; you can add custom flag definitions for your domain.
- **Outcomes** — How a conversation resolved (for example Resolved, Partially Resolved, or Failed), with custom outcome types available.
- **AI literacy** — A per-user measure of how effectively the end-user works with the chatbot.
- **Personas** — AI-generated user persona cards that match users to discovered patterns.

To tune accuracy, you add **calibration** examples for sentiment, intent, flags, outcomes, issues, and faithfulness so the pipeline matches your judgments.

## The four surfaces

Once analysis runs, you read the results through four main surfaces:

- **Overview dashboard** — The chatbot's home page combines summary cards (At-Risk Orgs, Performance, Activity, Risks) with an AI chat where you ask natural-language questions about your data.
- **Conversations and search** — Browse, filter, and open individual conversations with their full transcript and analysis tabs, or use Deep Search to find conversations by meaning instead of keywords.
- **Reports** — AI-native dashboards built by chatting with a copilot, started from templates like Customer Pulse or Sentiment Distribution, or from a blank report. Reports persist as reusable dashboards.
- **Workflows** — Automation rules that fire when analysis conditions are met, sending alerts to destinations such as Slack, Discord, or custom webhooks.
