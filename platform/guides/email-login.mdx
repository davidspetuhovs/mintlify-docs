Authenticate with your email and password to reach your chatbot analytics dashboard. This is for returning users who already have an OpenBat account.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - You have an existing OpenBat account.
  - You are not already logged in.
  - You know your email and password.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Navigate to `/auth/login`. The form shows a "Welcome back." heading with an **Email** field, a **Password** field, and a **Sign in** button.

    <Frame caption="Step 1: the login form with Email and Password fields visible">
      <img src="/images/guides/email-login/step-01.png" alt="email-login step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type your email address into the **Email** field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Type your password into the **Password** field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click the **Sign in** button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the redirect">
    After authentication completes, you are redirected to `/platform`, where the dashboard shows your organization and its chatbot list.

    <Frame caption="Step 5: the /platform dashboard showing the organization's chatbot list">
      <img src="/images/guides/email-login/step-02.png" alt="email-login step 5" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You are redirected to `/platform`, showing your organization's chatbot list.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sign in returns an 'Invalid email or password' error">
    Submitting the form with a wrong password shows an inline error in red: "Invalid email or password". The form stays on `/auth/login`. Re-check your email and password and try again, or use the **Forgot?** link to reset your password.

    <Frame caption="Inline error shown after submitting with a wrong password">
      <img src="/images/guides/email-login/failure-01.png" alt="email-login failure 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
