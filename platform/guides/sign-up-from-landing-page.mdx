Create an OpenBat account directly from the marketing homepage, without first navigating to the registration page. This path is for a visitor who does not yet have an OpenBat account and wants to start setting up chatbot analytics.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - You do not have an existing OpenBat account.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Land on the homepage">
    Open the homepage at openbat.dev. The hero section is visible with the 'Google Analytics for AI chatbots' heading.

    <Frame caption="The OpenBat homepage hero with the 'Google Analytics for AI chatbots' heading.">
      <img src="/images/guides/sign-up-from-landing-page/step-01.png" alt="sign-up-from-landing-page step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scroll through the feature sections">
    Scroll down to read the feature sections: the problem statement, ingest and analyze, system prompt suggestions, stakeholder notifications, analytics, and integration code.

    <Frame caption="Feature sections explaining ingest and analyze, prompt suggestions, notifications, and analytics.">
      <img src="/images/guides/sign-up-from-landing-page/step-02.png" alt="sign-up-from-landing-page step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Start listening'">
    Click the 'Start listening' button. It appears in the nav header, as a mid-page CTA, and as a bottom CTA. Clicking it opens an inline 'Get started' modal with a work email field, rather than redirecting to the registration page.

    <Frame caption="The inline 'Get started' modal with a work email field opens over the page.">
      <img src="/images/guides/sign-up-from-landing-page/step-03.png" alt="sign-up-from-landing-page step 3" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email and sign up">
    Enter your email in the modal and click 'Sign up'. The modal shows the confirmation: "You're in. Check your inbox; we'll be in touch with next steps shortly."

    <Frame caption="Confirmation message after submitting your email in the 'Get started' modal.">
      <img src="/images/guides/sign-up-from-landing-page/step-04.png" alt="sign-up-from-landing-page step 4" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You submitted your work email and saw the confirmation that you're in and should check your inbox for next steps.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Already logged in and 'Start listening' reopens the lead-capture modal">
    If you are already logged in and click 'Start listening', the same 'Get started' lead-capture modal opens rather than taking you to the platform. The nav also still shows the 'Sign in' button even when you are authenticated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
