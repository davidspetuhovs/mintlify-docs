Invite a colleague to join your organization by sending an email invitation, setting their organization role and chatbot access. This is for an organization owner or admin who wants to give a colleague access to chatbot analytics.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - You have the owner or admin role in the organization.
  - The invitee has an email address.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members page">
    Navigate to `/platform/members` from the organization-level sidebar, which sits alongside Chatbots and Settings. The member table shows current members with their Email, Role (Owner/Admin/Member), and Joined date.

    <Frame caption="The Members table listing current organization members and their roles.">
      <img src="/images/guides/invite-team-member/step-01.png" alt="invite-team-member step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the invite modal and configure the invitation">
    Click `+ Invite member`. A modal opens with an Email field, an Organization role dropdown (defaults to Member), Chatbot access checkboxes (Sophie, E-Commerce Demo, SaaS Demo) with a `Select all` option, and a chatbot role dropdown (defaults to Viewer).

    Enter the invitee's email address, select the organization role, choose which chatbots they can access, and set the chatbot role. Click `Send invitation` to send the invite, or `Cancel` to discard it.

    <Frame caption="The Invite member modal with email, organization role, chatbot access, and chatbot role.">
      <img src="/images/guides/invite-team-member/step-02.png" alt="invite-team-member step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  The invitation email is sent. The invitee clicks the link in the email to accept and join the organization.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Email already belongs to an existing member">
    If the email address you entered is already associated with a member of the organization, the invitation cannot be sent. Check the member table on the Members page to confirm whether the person already has access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invitation email goes to spam">
    The invitation email may land in the invitee's spam or junk folder. Ask the invitee to check those folders for the invite link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Role permissions insufficient">
    Sending invitations requires the owner or admin role. If you cannot invite members, confirm your role in the organization with an owner or admin.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
