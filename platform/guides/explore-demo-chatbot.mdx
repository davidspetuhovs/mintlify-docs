Open a read-only demo chatbot to explore the full OpenBat dashboard without needing real data. This is useful if you are evaluating OpenBat or want to see what a fully populated dashboard looks like.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - You are authenticated and signed in to OpenBat.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to /platform">
    Go to `/platform`. The chatbot list opens on the tab for your current organization's chatbots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the 'Demo' tab">
    Click the **Demo** tab to see demo chatbots. Two demo chatbots are visible: **Demo Chatbot — E-Commerce** (Threadworks Co.) and **Demo Chatbot — SaaS** (Helmsmith). Both are marked **Provided by OpenBat** with the note "Explore reports, browse the UI — no writes".

    <Frame caption="The Demo tab listing the E-Commerce and SaaS demo chatbots.">
      <img src="/images/guides/explore-demo-chatbot/step-01.png" alt="explore-demo-chatbot step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a demo chatbot">
    Click a demo chatbot card, such as the E-Commerce demo. The full dashboard loads with a DEMO banner, the AI chat, AT-RISK ORGS, ALERTS (13 buying signals), PERFORMANCE (Resolution 54%, Sentiment positive, 3 quality issues, AI Literacy 74/100), and full sidebar navigation.

    <Frame caption="The E-Commerce demo dashboard with the DEMO banner, AI chat, and metric cards.">
      <img src="/images/guides/explore-demo-chatbot/step-02.png" alt="explore-demo-chatbot step 3" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You see a fully populated dashboard with sample conversations, analyses, and reports — all read-only.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Demo data looks static or out of date">
    Demo chatbot data may not be real-time. The dashboard is populated with sample data for exploration, so values shown in the demo do not update like a live chatbot's would.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
