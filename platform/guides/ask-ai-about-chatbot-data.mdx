Ask the AI assistant on the chatbot dashboard a natural-language question about your chatbot's analytics, and get back relevant data, charts, and insights. This is for product managers who want quick answers without writing queries.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - The chatbot has captured conversations.
  - You are on the chatbot dashboard at `/platform/[chatbotId]`.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the message input field">
    On the dashboard, locate the AI chat message input field. Command hints next to it show the available scopes you can add to a question: `@` to tag an entity, `$` to scope by plan, `#` for user analysis, `!` for assistant analysis, and `^` to scope by prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question and submit">
    Type a natural-language question, for example `What's driving negative sentiment?`, then click **Submit**. The URL updates with a `?c=` conversation ID and your question appears in the chat view.

    <Frame caption="Asking the AI assistant a natural-language question from the dashboard chat input.">
      <img src="/images/guides/ask-ai-about-chatbot-data/step-01.png" alt="ask-ai-about-chatbot-data step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
The AI responds with relevant data, charts, and insights based on the chatbot's conversation data.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The question appears but no response comes back">
    If the AI backend returns an error, the failure appears silently: your question shows in the chat, but no AI response, error message, or loading-timeout indicator appears, leaving the chat empty. If the chat stays empty, try submitting the question again or rephrasing it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
