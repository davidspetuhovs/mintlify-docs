Find a conversation, open its full message thread, and read the analysis results so you understand what happened. This is how a CS manager investigates a flagged interaction to see why a customer expressed frustration.

<Note title="Before you begin">
- Conversations exist in the chatbot.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Conversations list">
    Navigate to `/platform/[chatbotId]/conversations`. The table lists conversations with User, Messages, Outcome, Issues, Sentiment, Flags, and Last Message columns. Use the search/filter bar to narrow results, and adjust the date filter (defaults to Last 30 days) as needed.

    <Frame caption="The Conversations table with User, Messages, Outcome, Issues, Sentiment, Flags, and Last Message columns.">
      <img src="/images/guides/review-a-specific-conversation/step-01.png" alt="review-a-specific-conversation step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a conversation and read its analysis">
    Click a conversation row to open the detail page. The full message thread loads, with the user message and the assistant response — including expandable Reasoning and tool call sections, and the formatted answer. The right panel shows the Analysis Overview (flag and outcome), a Persona section, and General, User, and Assistant tabs.

    <Frame caption="The conversation detail page: full message thread on the left, Analysis Overview with flags, outcome, and persona on the right.">
      <img src="/images/guides/review-a-specific-conversation/step-02.png" alt="review-a-specific-conversation step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You can read the full context of the conversation, including its analysis tags, flags, and outcomes.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No conversations match the filter criteria">
    The search term, filters, or date range may exclude every conversation. Clear the search/filter bar and widen the date filter (it defaults to Last 30 days) to see more rows.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
