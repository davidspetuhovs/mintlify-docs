Start a new report from a pre-built template to get a populated dashboard of relevant widgets without building from scratch. This suits a CS manager who wants a recurring sentiment overview.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - Your chatbot has conversation data.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports page">
    Navigate to the chatbot's **Reports** page (`/platform/[chatbotId]/reports`). The **START FROM A TEMPLATE** section lists four templates — Customer Pulse, Sentiment Distribution, Risk Flags Leaderboard, and Topics & Intents Overview. The **Your reports** section shows **No reports yet** until you create one.

    <Frame caption="The Reports page with the START FROM A TEMPLATE section and an empty Your reports list.">
      <img src="/images/guides/create-report-from-template/step-01.png" alt="create-report-from-template step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a template card">
    Click the **Customer Pulse** template card to create a report from it.

    <Frame caption="Selecting the Customer Pulse template card.">
      <img src="/images/guides/create-report-from-template/step-02.png" alt="create-report-from-template step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the created report">
    The report is created and loaded with pre-configured widgets. The Customer Pulse report shows CONVERSATIONS, AVG SENTIMENT, TOTAL MESSAGES, and SENTIMENT TREND. Filters default to **Last 30 days** and Prompt **All versions**. Report actions include **+ Add chart**, **Customize**, **Pin**, and **Delete**, plus a **Chat** button for the copilot. Optionally customize the report by chatting with the copilot sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
A new report is created with pre-populated widgets showing relevant chatbot analytics.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Widgets appear empty">
    A template can show empty widgets if your chatbot has no data for that analysis type. Confirm the chatbot has conversation data for the metrics the template tracks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
