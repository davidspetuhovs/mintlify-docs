Request a password reset email when you can no longer remember your password. This flow is for an existing user with a verified email who needs a one-time link to set a new password.

<Note title="Before you begin">
  - You have an existing account with a verified email.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the forgot-password page">
    Navigate to `/auth/forgot-password`, or click the **Forgot?** link on the sign-in page. The page shows a **Forgot your password?** heading with an **Email** field, a **Send reset link** button, and a **Back to sign in** link.

    <Frame caption="The forgot-password page with the Email field and Send reset link button">
      <img src="/images/guides/password-reset-request/forgot-password.png" alt="Forgot password page" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Step 1: the Forgot your password? form">
      <img src="/images/guides/password-reset-request/step-01.png" alt="password-reset-request step 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit your email">
    Enter the email address associated with your account in the **Email** field, then click **Send reset link**.

    <Frame caption="Step 2: enter your email and click Send reset link">
      <img src="/images/guides/password-reset-request/step-02.png" alt="password-reset-request step 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the reset link was sent">
    The page shows a **RESET LINK SENT** badge with the message: "If your email is registered, a reset link is on its way. The link expires in one hour."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the reset link and set a new password">
    Check your email for the reset link and click it to navigate to `/auth/update-password`. Enter your new password and confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
Your password is updated, and you can log in with the new password.
</Check>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You submitted an email that may not be registered">
    The page shows the same success message whether or not the email exists, for example: "If nonexistent@nobody.com is registered, a reset link is on its way." This is intentional — it does not reveal whether an account exists for that address.

    <Frame caption="The same success message appears for an unregistered email">
      <img src="/images/guides/password-reset-request/failure-01.png" alt="password-reset-request failure 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The reset link expired">
    The reset link expires one hour after it is sent. If it no longer works, return to `/auth/forgot-password` and request a new link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The email went to spam">
    If you do not see the reset email, check your spam or junk folder before requesting another link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
