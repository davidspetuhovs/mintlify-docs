Workflows turn conversation analysis results into automated alerts and actions. When specific analysis conditions are met during a conversation, a workflow fires and notifies an external destination such as Slack, Discord, or a custom webhook.

<Frame caption="The Workflows page with the Workflows, Runs, and Templates tabs.">
  <img src="/images/platform/workflows.png" alt="Workflows page" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Create a new workflow with the **Create workflow** button.
- Browse pre-built workflow **Templates**, categorized as Alerting, Escalation, Reporting, Moderation, and General.
- View workflow execution history on the **Runs** tab.
- Filter runs by status: All, Pending, Triggered, Skipped, Success, or Failed.
- Switch between the **Workflows**, **Runs**, and **Templates** tabs.

## Where it lives

Workflows live at `/platform/[chatbotId]/workflows`, accessed from the chatbot sidebar. Workflows connect analysis results to external actions, so you configure their destinations (Discord, Slack, custom webhooks) on the **Webhooks** tab of [Chatbot settings](/platform/features/chatbot-settings).
