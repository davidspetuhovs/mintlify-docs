The overview dashboard is the primary workspace for a chatbot, combining an AI chat for asking natural-language questions about your data with summary cards for health, performance, activity, and risk. It opens when you click a chatbot card from `/platform` and lives at `/platform/[chatbotId]`.

<Frame caption="The chatbot overview dashboard with the AI chat and summary cards">
  <img src="/images/platform/overview-dashboard.png" alt="Chatbot overview dashboard" />
</Frame>

## What you can do here

- Ask the AI chat any question about your chatbot data using the message input ("What can I help you discover?").
- Use command hints while typing: `@` to tag an entity, `$` to scope by plan, `#` for user analysis, `!` for assistant analysis, and `^` to scope by prompt.
- View at-risk organizations through the At-Risk Orgs card and the "View all" link.
- Check performance metrics: Resolution %, Quality Issues, and AI Literacy.
- Monitor activity trends through the conversation count on the Activity card.
- Review risk signals: churn risks, yes-man, and hallucinations.
- Click any metric to drill down, and read its sparkline chart.
- Reopen a past AI chat from the History dropdown.

## Summary cards

Each summary card surfaces a different dimension of chatbot health, and every metric is clickable with a sparkline chart.

- **At-Risk Orgs** — customer organizations flagged as at risk, with a "View all" link to the full directory.
- **Performance** — Resolution %, Quality Issues, and AI Literacy.
- **Activity** — conversation count over the period.
- **Risks** — churn risks, yes-man, and hallucinations.

## Where it lives

The dashboard is at `/platform/[chatbotId]`, reached by clicking a chatbot card from `/platform`. Selecting a chatbot switches the sidebar to chatbot-specific navigation: Dashboard, Reports, Workflows, Prompt, Users, Conversations, Query Types, Deep Search, Analysis Config, and Chatbot Settings.

<Note>
The dashboard reflects data once your chatbot has captured conversations and been onboarded. Asking the AI chat before any conversations are captured returns empty results.
</Note>
