OpenBat uses a multi-tenant organization model with role-based access. The **Members** and **Settings** pages — reached from the organization-level sidebar alongside **Chatbots** — let owners and admins manage who belongs to the organization and configure the organization itself.

## Organization members

The **Members** page lives at `/platform/members`. It shows a table of every organization member with **Email**, **Role**, and **Joined** columns, and supports inviting new people.

From this page you can:

- View the member list with each person's role (Owner, Admin, or Member)
- Invite a new member by email via **Invite member**
- Manage member roles

### Roles

Members hold one of three organization roles — **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Member**. Beyond the organization level, members can be given granular chatbot-level permissions (Editor or Viewer) when invited.

Set per-chatbot access for members on a chatbot's **Members** tab in **Chatbot settings**.
</Card>

## Inviting a team member

To add someone to the organization, open **Members** and choose **Invite member**. Enter the invitee's email address, select a role (admin or member), and submit. An invitation email is sent; the invitee clicks the link to accept and join the organization. Accepted invitations are handled at `/platform/invitations/accept`.

## Organization settings

Organization-level settings live at `/platform/settings`. This page is separate from chatbot settings — it controls the organization itself, not any individual chatbot.

From here you can:

- Edit the organization display name in the **Organization name** field and **Save**
- Delete the organization from the danger zone (destructive, requires confirmation)
